PRIVACY POLICY AI Food Business Prediction Effective Date: August 12, 2026 1. INTRODUCTION AI Food Business Prediction is an application designed to help users evaluate and predict the potential success of a food business using business and market-related information provided by the user. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the AI Food Business Prediction application. 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT The application may receive information entered by the user into the business prediction form. This may include: • Food business type • Business and market information • Location or market-related information entered by the user • Pricing information • Competition information • Demand-related information • Cost and affordability information • Growth-related information • Other information required to generate a prediction Users should not enter unnecessary personal or sensitive information into the application. 3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION Information submitted through the application may be used to: • Process food-business prediction requests • Generate prediction results • Calculate business scores • Evaluate potential business success • Provide recommendations • Provide risk information • Operate and maintain the application • Improve the reliability of the prediction service 4. BACKEND AND DATA TRANSMISSION The application communicates with its backend server through HTTPS/TLS. Information necessary to generate a prediction may be transmitted from the application to the backend server. The production backend is hosted on Render. Backend API: https://ai-food-businesses-sucess-prediction-2.onrender.com 5. DATA SHARING We do not sell users' personal information. Information submitted to the application may be transmitted to and processed by our backend service for the purpose of providing the prediction functionality. We do not intentionally share submitted information with advertisers for advertising purposes. Technical infrastructure providers may process information necessary to provide hosting and backend infrastructure services. 6. DATA STORAGE Prediction information may be processed by the backend service. If information is stored by the backend, it may be retained as necessary to operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, or improve the service. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information. 7. PERSONAL INFORMATION The application does not require users to create an account to use the prediction functionality. The application does not intentionally require: • Full name • Email address • Telephone number • Government identification • Contacts • Calendar information Users should not enter unnecessary personal information into prediction fields. 8. LOCATION INFORMATION The application does not automatically access the device's GPS location unless location functionality and the relevant permission are explicitly added. If a user manually enters location or market information, that information may be sent to the backend as part of the prediction request. 9. CAMERA, MICROPHONE, PHOTOS AND FILES The application does not require access to: • Camera • Microphone • Photos • Videos • Contacts • Calendar • Device files unless such functionality is added in a future version. 10. ADVERTISING AI Food Business Prediction does not currently display advertisements. The application does not currently use Google AdMob or another advertising SDK for advertising purposes. 11. ANALYTICS AND CRASH REPORTING The application does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or analytics purposes. If analytics or crash-reporting services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate. 12. SECURITY The application uses HTTPS/TLS when communicating with the production backend. We take reasonable technical measures to protect information transmitted through the application. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. 13. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY The application is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. 14. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES The application uses third-party infrastructure services necessary to operate the backend. The production backend is hosted using Render. Third-party providers may process technical information according to their applicable terms and privacy policies. 15. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION The application does not currently require users to create accounts. Because there is no user account system, there is currently no in-app account deletion feature.
